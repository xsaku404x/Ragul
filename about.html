About Siddha System

Siddha medicine is one of the oldest traditional systems of medicine in India, originating in Tamil Nadu. It is based on natural healing methods using herbs, minerals, and lifestyle practices to maintain balance in the body.

Philosophy

The Siddha system believes that the human body is made up of five basic elements: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space. Health is achieved by maintaining balance between these elements and the three humors (Vatham, Pitham, and Kabam).

Treatments

  • Herbal medicines
  • Diet and lifestyle guidance
  • Varma Therapy
  • Natural detoxification methods

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote natural healing and wellness through authentic Siddha medicine, combining traditional knowledge with modern understanding.
